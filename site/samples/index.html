A few things to know.

Select Atolls on the FM-1 before saving or removing samples. Keep power and USB connected while writing to memory. Each slot can be used by A, B or both; choose the sources on the instrument’s Sample page.

Use Chrome or Edge with Web MIDI support. WAVs are decoded, trimmed, normalized and auditioned in your browser. They are not uploaded to a server. The preview includes the slot’s ADPCM encoding, so you can hear the prepared format before saving. The recorded sample in a slot cannot be downloaded or auditioned from this page; preview is available for a clip you prepare here.

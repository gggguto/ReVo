Bring Bioma and Atolls to your M-VAVE FM-1.

REVO 1.0 / BUILD 2026110607

Install over USB.

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and connect your FM-1 with a data USB cable.
  2. Close other MIDI applications. Connect below, then choose Install ReVo.
  3. Keep power and USB connected until the update finishes and the instrument returns.

ReVo is an independent, open-source firmware. Installing it replaces your current firmware. Keep a copy of anything you want to preserve.

Your FM-1 will only be accessed after you connect.

Your first visit.

Bioma opens by default. Press PLAY/STOP to awaken its inhabitants. To visit Atolls, hold HOME → Machine → Atolls → SEL.

If an update is interrupted

Keep power connected. Reconnect USB and retry the same package if the update loader is waiting. For ReVo rescue mode, hold OCT− while powering on, then connect again.

If your computer only sees WL82 UBOOT, MIDI recovery is unavailable in this installer. Consult the community recovery documentation.
